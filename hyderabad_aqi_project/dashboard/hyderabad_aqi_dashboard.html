🌿 Air Quality Trend
AQI fell from 137.7 in 2015 to 78.2 in 2020 — a 43% improvement over 5 years. Policy interventions and industrial regulation contributed to this decline.
📅 Seasonality Insight
Winter months (Oct–Jan) show highest AQI (~125–137). Monsoon season (Jun–Aug) brings cleanest air (79–91), likely due to rain washing out particulates.
⚠ Health Risk Days
72 days exceeded AQI 200 (Poor to Severe), representing 3.6% of all days. Sensitive groups should avoid outdoor exposure during these episodes.
